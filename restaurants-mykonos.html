Mykonos · DiningThe best restaurants in Mykonos

The right table, at the right time, in the most sought after dining rooms of the island.

Dinner in Mykonos is an event: late, long and often festive. From the hidden gardens of the town to the fish tavernas by the water, the best tables are booked well in advance in July and August. We know where to go for each evening and we secure the table you want, at the time you want.

Our favourite restaurants

Interni. A hidden garden in the heart of Matoyianni, elegant and lively, one of the classic addresses of the island.

Lío. In Mykonos town, dinner and cabaret in one evening, with performers and music that turn the meal into a show.

Nōema. In Mykonos town, a stylish address mixing restaurant, bar and boutique, with a refined take on Aegean flavours.

Mamalouka. In Mykonos town, a warm and festive table, much loved for long dinners with friends.

Buddha-Bar Beach. At the Santa Marina resort in Ornos, Asian fusion cuisine and lounge music facing the bay.

Zuma. Contemporary Japanese cuisine, chic and buzzing, for a lively evening in town.

Bill & Coo. At Megali Ammos, a clifftop terrace with one of the most beautiful sunsets over the Aegean.

Spilia. At Agia Anna, seafood served in a sea cave, with your feet almost in the water.

Gioia. Elegant Italian cuisine in a chic and lively atmosphere.

Greek flavours

Fresh fish, grilled octopus, Mykonian cheeses and local wines: we also know the more authentic tables loved by the islanders.

When to book

In July and August, the most popular restaurants are booked one to two weeks ahead. Dinner is late in Mykonos, rarely before 9.30 pm.

After dinner

In Mykonos, dinner often flows into the night. We can arrange your table at the club afterwards and the chauffeur to take you there.

What we arrange

  • Tables at the most requested restaurants, even in August
  • The right time and the best table
  • Birthday cakes, champagne and special requests
  • Private dinners at your villa with a chef
  • Your chauffeur and your club table afterwards

Questions about restaurants in Mykonos

How far ahead should I book?

In July and August, one to two weeks ahead for the most famous restaurants. Tell us your dates and we handle it, including last minute requests.

What time do people dine in Mykonos?

Late. Most restaurants fill up from 9.30 pm and the atmosphere peaks around 11 pm.

Is there a dress code?

Chic but relaxed. Linen, white and elegant summer outfits are the norm in the best restaurants.

Can you book for a large group?

Yes. We secure large tables, set menus or a private area depending on the restaurant.

Can you organise a dinner at our villa?

Yes. A private chef, a waiter and a table set by the pool, with a menu designed for you.
