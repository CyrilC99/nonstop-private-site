Journal · DubaiDubai in winterThe guide

Updated November 2026 · 7 min read

While Europe turns grey, Dubai enters its finest season. Warm days, mild evenings, a calendar full of events and a city that never says no. Here is how to make the most of it.

Why Dubai is at its best in winter

From November to April, days are sunny and warm without the summer heat, and evenings are perfect for dinners outdoors. It is the season when the city lives outside: terraces, beach clubs, rooftops and the desert.

It is also the busiest season. The weeks around Christmas and New Year are the most sought after of the year, with the fireworks of the Burj Khalifa on the night of the 31st. For these dates, the best suites, villas and tables are booked weeks or even months ahead.

Where to stay

Palm Jumeirah. Beachfront villas and resorts with private beaches, a short drive from everything. Ideal for families and for those who want the sea at their door.

Downtown. Penthouses and suites facing the Burj Khalifa, close to the best restaurants and shopping. Perfect for a city stay.

Dubai Marina and JBR. Lively, by the water, with the marina at your feet and yachts a few minutes away.

Jumeirah. Quieter, with low rise villas and beautiful beaches between the old town and the Burj Al Arab.

Days by the sea

Winter is the season of beach clubs: long lunches on a daybed, the sea at a perfect temperature and the skyline in the distance. The best ones fill up quickly at weekends, so a reservation makes all the difference.

For a change of rhythm, a day at a resort spa or an afternoon of golf on one of the championship courses is never far away.

The desert, the other Dubai

Less than an hour from the city, the dunes offer a completely different experience. A private dinner under the stars, a sunrise flight in a hot air balloon, falconry or a drive across the sand with an experienced guide.

Winter is the ideal season: the nights are cool and the light at sunset is magnificent.

Yachts, tables and nights

A sunset cruise along the Palm and the skyline is one of the most beautiful ways to see the city. For a full day, the yacht leaves from the marina with lunch on board and a swim stop off the coast.

In the evening, Dubai has some of the most coveted tables in the world, from rooftops to chef restaurants. On Fridays and Saturdays, the best addresses are booked well ahead.

Beyond Dubai

Abu Dhabi is around an hour and a half away by car, with the Louvre Abu Dhabi and the Formula 1 Grand Prix at the end of the season. For a change of scenery, the mountains of Hatta offer lakes, hiking and a cooler climate.

We arrange everything for your stay: villas and hotels · yachts · private jets and helicopters · tables and nights · chauffeurs.

Questions about Dubai in winter

What is the best time to visit Dubai?

From November to April, when days are warm and sunny and evenings are mild. December and January are the busiest months.

When should I book for Christmas and New Year in Dubai?

As early as possible, ideally several months ahead. The best suites, villas and tables for New Year’s Eve are the first to go.

Where should I stay in Dubai?

Palm Jumeirah for the beach and families, Downtown for the city and the Burj Khalifa, Dubai Marina for a lively stay by the water, Jumeirah for calm.

Can you organise a private dinner in the desert?

Yes. We arrange private dinners in the dunes, sunrise balloon flights, falconry and desert drives with experienced guides.

Can I rent a yacht in Dubai for a few hours?

Yes. Sunset cruises of a few hours are very popular, and full days at sea are possible with lunch on board.
