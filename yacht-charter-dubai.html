Dubai · YachtingYacht charter in Dubai

Palm Jumeirah, the skyline at sunset and nights at sea, with captain and crew.

Dubai is a city best seen from the sea. In a few hours on a private yacht you pass the Palm, the Burj Al Arab and the towers of the Marina, swim in calm warm water and watch the skyline light up at night. We find the boat, the crew and the timing that suit your group.

Which yacht for your day

Sport yachts. Agile and fast, perfect for a few hours along the coast for a small group.

Motor yachts. From 25 to 40 metres, with a large sundeck, cabins and a crew. The classic choice for a full afternoon or a sunset cruise.

Superyachts. For a birthday, a corporate evening or a large group, with a full crew, chef and DJ on request.

The classic routes

Palm Jumeirah and Atlantis. The full crescent of the Palm, with a swim stop in its calm waters.

Burj Al Arab and Jumeirah. The iconic sail shaped hotel seen from the water, then the beaches of Jumeirah.

Dubai Marina and Ain Dubai. The towers of the Marina and Bluewaters, magical at sunset.

The World Islands. A longer outing offshore, for a quieter day at anchor.

Life on board

Your crew takes care of everything: drinks, towels, water toys, music and a meal served on deck. Seafood platter, sushi, a cake for a birthday: tell us what you like and it is waiting on board.

When to go

The best season runs from October to April, with warm sea and soft evenings. In summer the heat is intense: we favour early mornings, late afternoons and night cruises.

The skyline at night

End the day facing the lights of the Marina and the Palm, with dinner on board or a transfer straight to your restaurant.

What we arrange

  • The yacht that suits your group and your occasion
  • Captain, crew, catering and drinks on board
  • Water toys, jet skis and seabobs
  • Decoration, DJ or photographer for a celebration
  • Transfers from your hotel to the marina

Questions about yacht charter in Dubai

Where do the yachts leave from?

Mostly from Dubai Marina and Dubai Harbour, a few minutes from the Palm. We choose the marina closest to your hotel.

How long is a typical charter?

Most guests book between three and five hours. A full day or a night cruise is also possible.

Can we swim during the trip?

Yes. Your captain stops in calm water near the Palm or the islands, with the crew and water toys ready.

Can you organise a birthday or a proposal on board?

Yes. Decoration, cake, champagne, music and a photographer, all prepared before you step on board.

How far ahead should I book?

From November to March and around New Year’s Eve, as early as possible. The rest of the year, a few days are often enough.
