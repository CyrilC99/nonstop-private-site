Saint-Tropez · NightlifeNightlife in Saint-Tropez

Dinners that turn into parties, legendary clubs and the best tables until sunrise.

In Saint-Tropez, the night starts at dinner and ends at sunrise. A festive restaurant on the port, then the most famous club of the Riviera, with the yachts lit up in the background. In July and August, the tables are taken fast. We know the venues and we get you the right table, at the right time.

Where the night happens

Les Caves du Roy. At the Byblos, the legendary club of Saint-Tropez, with top DJs and a celebrity crowd.

L’Opéra. On the port, dinner with performances that turns into a party facing the yachts.

Papagayo. A trendy address where the night goes on until the early hours.

Le Quai. On the harbour, chic and lively, with live music and DJs.

Dinner then party

Bagatelle. The festive spirit of the house, from dinner to dancing.

Gaïo. A chic dinner that carries on into the night.

Cherry. Between restaurant and club, for a night that never stops.

The rhythm of the night

Aperitif on the port around 8 pm, dinner from 9 pm, the festive restaurants rise after 11 pm and the clubs fill up after midnight. Your chauffeur follows you from one stop to the next.

When to go

July and August are the peak of the season, with guest DJs and special nights. June and September are calmer, with the same magic.

Your table

VIP tables come with a minimum spend that depends on the club and the night. We confirm the conditions in advance, choose the best spot and handle your arrival.

What we arrange

  • The best tables, even in August
  • Festive dinners that carry on into the night
  • Minimum spend and conditions explained in advance
  • Your chauffeur from the villa and back
  • Birthdays and celebrations, arranged with the venue

Questions about nightlife in Saint-Tropez

Which is the most famous club in Saint-Tropez?

Les Caves du Roy, at the Byblos, the legendary club of the Riviera.

Is there a minimum spend?

Yes, VIP tables come with a minimum spend that depends on the club and the night. We confirm it before booking.

When should I book?

For July and August, as early as possible, especially on weekends and for big DJ nights.

Is there a dress code?

Elegant summer outfits. A table reservation makes the door much easier.

Can you arrange transport at night?

Yes. Your chauffeur takes you from the villa to dinner and the club, and back whenever you want.
