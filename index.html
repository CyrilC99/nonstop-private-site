About us

Built on one idea: you should never have to wait.

NONSTOP PRIVATE was born on the ground, between private villas, yachts, clubs and airports in the world's most demanding destinations. It started with a simple observation: the people we look after do not need more options. They need someone who answers, who knows, and who gets it done.

So we kept it simple. One dedicated contact, reachable at any hour, in any time zone. No call centre, no catalogue, no endless back-and-forth. Just a message, a precise proposal and a request handled from start to finish.

Behind that contact stands a network of trusted partners across private aviation, yachting, villas and hotels, the best tables and discreet security teams. Discretion is not a service we offer; it is simply how we work.

Anything. Anywhere. Any time. The promise behind the name.
