Mykonos · Beach clubsThe best beach clubs in Mykonos

Front row sunbeds, long lunches by the sea and the most famous sunsets of the Aegean.

In Mykonos, the day happens at the beach club. Lunch turns into a party at Psarou, the sun sets to music at Paraga, the north coast offers a more bohemian escape. In July and August, the best sunbeds and tables are booked days in advance. We know every club and we get you the spot you want.

Festive or bohemian

For the party. Nammos on Psarou beach and SantAnna at Paraga, where champagne flows and the afternoon turns into dancing.

For a bohemian escape. Scorpios at Paraga, Alemagou at Ftelia and Principote at Panormos, for a more relaxed and stylish atmosphere.

Which beach for which mood

The south coast. Psarou, Platis Gialos, Paraga and Paradise, sheltered from the meltemi and the liveliest in summer.

The north coast. Ftelia, Panormos and Agios Sostis, wilder and more windswept, with a bohemian spirit.

Super Paradise and beyond. JackieO’ Beach and the clubs of the east coast, for those who want music from noon.

The long lunch

Lunch is the highlight of a Mykonos beach club: grilled fish, seafood pasta, Greek salads and chilled rosé, at a table on the sand that can last all afternoon.

When to go

The season runs from late May to early October. July and August are the busiest months; June and September offer the same magic with a little more space.

The sunset ritual

Some clubs are famous for their sunset, with live music and the sun dropping into the sea. Stay until the end, then head to dinner in town.

What we arrange

  • Front row sunbeds and tables, even in August
  • The club that suits your group and your mood
  • Minimum spend and conditions explained in advance
  • Arrival by boat or with your chauffeur
  • Your dinner reservation for the evening

Questions about beach clubs in Mykonos

How far ahead should I book a sunbed?

In July and August, as early as possible, ideally a week ahead for the most famous clubs. In June and September, a few days are often enough.

Is there a minimum spend?

Most clubs ask for a minimum spend per sunbed or table in high season. We confirm the conditions before booking.

Which beach clubs are the most festive?

Nammos and SantAnna are known for their party afternoons, while Scorpios is famous for its sunset atmosphere.

Can we arrive by boat?

Yes. Many clubs on the south coast can be reached by tender from your yacht. We coordinate the arrival with the club.

What about the wind?

When the meltemi blows, the south coast stays sheltered. We adapt the choice of club to the conditions.
