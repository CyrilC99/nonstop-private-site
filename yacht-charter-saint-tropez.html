Saint-Tropez · YachtingYacht charter in Saint-Tropez

Day trips, sunset cruises and weekly charters, with captain and crew.

In summer, the best way to live Saint-Tropez is from the sea. A yacht takes you to lunch at Pampelonne without the traffic, to the wild coves of the islands and back to the port for the evening. We find the right yacht and the right crew for your group, and we plan the day around your tables and your mood.

Which yacht for your day

Day boats and Rivas. Fast and elegant, perfect for a few hours along the coast or a transfer to a beach club.

Motor yachts. Comfortable and spacious, with crew, for a full day at sea with lunch on board.

Superyachts. For a week along the Riviera, with chef, cabins and water toys.

Where to go

Pampelonne. Anchor off the beach, then the tender takes you to lunch at your beach club.

Porquerolles and the Îles d’Hyères. Clear water, pine covered islands and quiet coves.

Cannes, Antibes and Monaco. For a longer cruise along the coast, from one legendary port to another.

Life on board

Swimming, paddle boards and water toys, a lunch prepared by the crew or a table booked ashore, champagne at sunset: every day is planned to your rhythm.

When to go

From May to September, with July and August at the heart of the season. Book early for August and for the Monaco Grand Prix and the Cannes Film Festival in May.

Sunset at sea

End the day at anchor off the Citadelle, watching the sun set over the gulf, then return to the port for dinner in the village.

What we arrange

  • The right yacht and crew for your group
  • The route, the moorings and the tender to the beach clubs
  • Your table ashore for lunch or dinner
  • Catering, decoration and celebrations on board
  • Helicopter or chauffeur to and from the port

Questions about yacht charter in Saint-Tropez

Do I need a licence?

No. Every charter we arrange comes with a professional captain and crew.

How far ahead should I book?

For July and August, as early as possible, ideally several weeks ahead. In June and September, a few days are often enough.

Can we go to a beach club by boat?

Yes. We anchor off Pampelonne and the tender takes you to the beach club, where your table is ready.

Where do the yachts leave from?

From the port of Saint-Tropez, Port Grimaud or Sainte-Maxime, depending on the yacht and your villa.

Can you organise a celebration on board?

Yes. Birthdays, proposals or private parties at sea, with decoration, music and catering.
