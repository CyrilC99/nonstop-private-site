About us

Anything. Anywhere. Any time.

NONSTOP PRIVATE is a luxury concierge and lifestyle management service. One message is all it takes: a single dedicated contact arranges your villa, your yacht, your private jet, your chauffeur, your bookings, then follows you on site until you leave.

What we arrange

Stays. Luxury villas, ski chalets, suites and palace hotels, staffed and ready on arrival.

On the water and in the air. Yacht charters with crew and chef, private jets and helicopter transfers.

On the ground. Private chauffeurs, luxury car rental and close protection.

Bookings. The most sought after restaurants, beach clubs and nightclubs, with the best tables.

Events and experiences. Formula 1 Grand Prix, New Year’s Eve, the Cannes Film Festival, private dinners, desert evenings and more.

Where we operate

Our main destinations are Dubai, Mykonos, Courchevel, the French Riviera with Saint-Tropez, Monaco and Cannes, and Ibiza. Through a network of trusted partners, we also arrange stays in Paris, London, Marbella, Miami, St Barth, Bali and anywhere our clients travel.

How we work

A message on WhatsApp, a call or the form is enough. A person answers, day or night, and comes back with a short, curated proposal and a precise quote. Nothing is booked until you approve it. Then we book, confirm and follow up on site. See how it works.

Discretion

Your details are only shared with the providers needed for your booking and are never used for anything else. A confidentiality agreement can be signed on request.

Membership

Clients can send a single request at any time. For those who travel often, our yearly membership adds a dedicated lifestyle manager, priority on every request and privileged access.
