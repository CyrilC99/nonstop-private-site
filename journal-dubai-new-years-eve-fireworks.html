Journal · DubaiNew Year’s Eve fireworksWhere to watch them in Dubai

Updated November 2026 · 6 min read

Every 31 December, Dubai lights up dozens of fireworks shows across the city, from the Burj Khalifa to Palm Jumeirah. The real luxury is having the right seat at midnight. Here is where to watch them, and how to book.

Burj Khalifa and Downtown

The most famous show in the city, launched from the tallest tower in the world. Downtown roads close from the afternoon and the crowd is huge, so the best way to see it is from a table with a view, booked well in advance.

The rooftops and restaurants facing the tower are the most requested of the night, like CÉ LA VI on the roof of Address Sky View. Dinner, the countdown, and the party right after.

Palm Jumeirah and Burj Al Arab

Atlantis The Palm, Atlantis The Royal and several hotels on the Palm launch their own fireworks over the sea. The atmosphere is more relaxed: dinner by the beach, then the sky lights up above the water.

The Burj Al Arab and Bluewaters also host their own shows. For New Year 2026, Dubai announced 48 fireworks displays across 40 locations, so wherever you are, there is a show nearby.

From a yacht

To see several shows at once, nothing beats the sea. Off the Palm or facing the Burj Al Arab, a private yacht with its crew lets you watch the fireworks away from the crowds, with dinner on board. Yachts for the 31st are among the first things to go.

After midnight

Then the night carries on in the clubs. Ushuaïa Dubai Harbour has announced its New Year’s Eve night on Thursday 31 December, and BLU, BUND, NYX and Raspoutine are among the most requested tables of the night.

On the 31st, tables are booked in advance and the best ones go first. We arrange the table, the arrival time and the driver.

How we plan your night

Road closures, traffic and crowds make the logistics of the night as important as the table. We plan the whole evening: dinner with a view, a driver who knows the open routes, the club after midnight, or a yacht for the full night.

Questions about New Year’s Eve in Dubai

Where can you watch the New Year’s Eve fireworks in Dubai?

The main shows are at the Burj Khalifa in Downtown, on Palm Jumeirah, at the Burj Al Arab and at Bluewaters, with many more across the city.

How many fireworks shows are there in Dubai on 31 December?

For New Year 2026, Dubai announced 48 fireworks displays across 40 locations. The official list for each year is published in December.

Can you watch the fireworks from a yacht?

Yes. A private yacht off the Palm or facing the Burj Al Arab is one of the best ways to see several shows away from the crowds. Book as early as possible.

When should I book a table for New Year’s Eve in Dubai?

As early as possible. Tables with a view of the Burj Khalifa and the best club tables are the first to go.
