Courchevel · NightlifeNightlife in Courchevel

Après ski with DJs, festive dinners and the best tables until the end of the night.

In Courchevel, the night starts on the slopes. Après ski with a DJ in the last sun, dinner that turns into a party, then the club until the early hours. During the festive weeks and in February, the tables are taken fast. We know the venues and we get you the right table, at the right time.

Where the night happens

Les Caves de Courchevel. The legendary club of the resort for decades, where every night of the season ends.

Bagatelle. Festive dinners with music, where the party starts at the table.

La Mangeoire. Dinner, then the piano bar and a party that goes on until late.

Le Kalinka. Festive and generous, where dinner turns into a celebration.

La Grange. A glamorous club in the centre of 1850, with top DJs and an elegant crowd.

Le Bar des Airelles. The lavish bar of the palace, for a drink before or after dinner.

Après ski

Le Cap Horn. A sunny terrace near the altiport, with music and champagne at the end of the day.

La Folie Douce. At La Saulire, between Courchevel and Méribel, the most famous party on the slopes, with shows and DJs.

La Soucoupe. A festive terrace on the slopes, where lunch turns into a party.

Loulou. On the slopes at the Barrière Les Neiges palace, a lively lunch that goes on into the afternoon.

L’Apogée Snow Lounge. Fire pits, cocktails and sun decks, for a more elegant après ski.

The village at night

Courchevel 1850 lives at night between the palaces, the bars of the hotels and the clubs, all within a few minutes of each other. Your chauffeur waits at the door and takes you back to the chalet whenever you want.

The festive weeks

Christmas, New Year and the Russian Orthodox New Year in January are the highlights of the season, with guest DJs and special nights. Tables for these dates are booked well in advance.

Your table

Most clubs ask for a minimum spend per table. We confirm the conditions in advance, choose the best spot in the room and handle your arrival.

What we arrange

  • The best tables, even for New Year

  • Festive dinners that carry on into the night
  • Minimum spend and conditions explained in advance

Questions about nightlife in Courchevel

Which is the most famous club in Courchevel?

Les Caves de Courchevel, the legendary club of the resort, where the nights of the season end.

Is there a minimum spend for a table?

Yes, most clubs ask for a minimum spend per table, higher during the festive weeks. We confirm the conditions before booking.

When should I book for New Year?

As early as possible. Tables for New Year’s Eve are the most sought after of the season.

Where is the best après ski?

Le Cap Horn, La Soucoupe and Loulou in Courchevel, La Folie Douce at La Saulire, and L’Apogée Snow Lounge for a more elegant mood.

Can you arrange transport at night?

Yes. Your chauffeur takes you from the chalet to dinner and the club, and back whenever you want.
