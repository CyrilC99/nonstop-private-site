Dubai · ExperiencesThe best experiences in Dubai

Helicopter over the Palm, private dinner in the desert, balloon at sunrise: Dubai, privately.

Beyond the restaurants and the beach clubs, Dubai offers experiences found nowhere else: the city seen from the sky, a dinner alone in the dunes, a balloon floating above the desert at sunrise. We organise them privately, at the right time, with the right partners.

From the sky

Helicopter. A private flight over the Palm, the Burj Al Arab and Downtown, from a few minutes to an hour.

Hot air balloon. Above the dunes at sunrise, followed by a breakfast in the desert.

Skydiving. A tandem jump over the Palm, one of the most spectacular drop zones in the world.

Seaplane. A flight along the coast, from the Creek to the Palm.

The desert

Private dinner in the dunes. A table for two or for your group, lanterns, a chef and the stars, with no one else around.

Falconry. A morning with a falconer, one of the oldest traditions of the Emirates.

Dune drive and camels. In a private 4x4 at sunset, at your own pace.

In the city

The Burj Khalifa at sunset. The highest observation deck, with private access to avoid the queues.

Dubai Opera and the big shows. The best seats for concerts and performances.

Museum of the Future. A private visit of the most futuristic building of the city.

When to go

From November to April for the desert and the outdoor experiences. Sunrise and sunset are the most beautiful moments.

Celebrations

A proposal in the desert, a birthday in a helicopter, a family dinner in the dunes: we design the experience around your moment.

What we arrange

  • Private helicopter flights and balloon rides
  • Private dinners in the desert
  • Falconry, dune drives and desert mornings
  • The best seats for shows and events
  • Your chauffeur from the hotel and back

Questions about experiences in Dubai

What is the most memorable experience in Dubai?

A private dinner in the desert or a helicopter flight over the Palm are among the most loved by our clients.

When is the best time?

From November to April. Sunrise for the balloon, sunset for the desert and the Burj Khalifa.

Can you organise a proposal?

Yes. In the desert, in a helicopter or on a yacht, with decoration, music and a photographer.

Are the experiences private?

Yes. We organise private versions whenever possible, just for you and your group.

Are children welcome?

Most experiences are suitable for families. We adapt the choice to your group.
